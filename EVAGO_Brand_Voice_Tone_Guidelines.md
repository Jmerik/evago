**Role:** Elite Brand Strategist & Master Copywriter  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Brand Name:** EVAGO  
**Based on:** EVAGO Business Plan — General Conference & Business Travel Audience

## Brand Archetype & Personality

EVAGO is the **calm, professional assistant** who quietly organizes your entire event week — so you arrive focused, on time, and always in the right room. We communicate with the clarity of a trusted colleague who has already checked the details and is now simply handing you the plan.

**Adjectives:** reliable, clear, quietly capable.

## Core Vocabulary

### Words We Love

These words reflect the practical, professional, and supportive nature of EVAGO. They are concrete and immediately useful.

| Category | Words |
|---|---|
| **Travel & Logistics** | itinerary, route, leg, segment, corridor, hub, transfer, booking, boarding, seat, departure, arrival, schedule, confirmation, check-in, pass, ticket, reservation, dispatch, vehicle |
| **Events & Venues** | event, venue, conference, session, side event, meeting, agenda, calendar, room, stage, forum, summit, week |
| **Assistance & Clarity** | assistant, support, help, plan, organize, ready, clear, simple, update, notification, reminder, summary, dashboard |
| **Status & Trust** | confirmed, updated, ready, on time, delayed, rebooked, secure, verified, accurate, reliable, protected, included |

### Banned Buzzwords

These words are banned because they sound like generic AI copy, empty startup pitch language, or crypto terminology that no longer applies to the EVAGO brand.

| Banned Word / Phrase | Why It Is Banned |
|---|---|
| **seamless** | Conference logistics are never truly seamless. We reduce friction; we do not pretend it disappears. |
| **revolutionary** | Overused. EVAGO improves logistics; it does not claim to change the world. |
| **disruptive** | Startup-bro vocabulary. We are not disrupting — we are organizing. |
| **leverage** | Corporate jargon. Replace with "use" or "take advantage of." |
| **unlock** | Implies hidden value or gamification. We simply open the right door. |
| **synergy** | Meaningless. |
| **scalable** | Internal engineering word, not a customer promise. |
| **optimized** | Sounds like an algorithm talking about people. |
| **frictionless** | False promise. We reduce steps; borders and queues still exist. |
| **AI-powered** | Unless a specific AI feature is proven and valuable, this is a red flag. |
| **world-class / best-in-class** | Empty. Show the quality instead. |
| **ecosystem** | Too broad. We are an event-week travel assistant. |
| **streamline** | Corporate verb. Replace with "simplify" or "organize." |
| **innovative** | Lazy. Show what is new instead of claiming it. |
| **cutting-edge** | Hyperbolic and unprofessional. |
| **end-to-end** | Implies infinite scope. We are specific about what we cover. |
| **holistic** | Wellness language, not logistics. |
| **next-gen / paradigm** | Unnecessary grandiosity. |
| **crypto, web3, blockchain, decentralized, NFT, token, protocol** | EVAGO is a business-travel platform. These terms have no place in the brand. |
| **swarm, fixer, node, hacker house** | Leftover from a crypto-native identity. No longer applicable. |
| **wallet** | Only acceptable when referring to the EVAGO Travel Pass or payment wallet. Never in a crypto context. |

## Voice in Action

> **Your conference week. One pass. Every event, every route, every QR code.**

### Subheadline

> EVAGO organizes your travel, side events, and tickets into one clear itinerary — so you can focus on the meetings that matter.

### Social Media Hook

> **No more spreadsheets, scattered bookings, and missed connections. Just your full event week, in one place.**

### Push Notification — TravelPass Ready

> **Your EVAGO Travel Pass is ready. All your event tickets, transit routes, and QR codes are in one place.**

### Push Notification — Delay Rebooking

> **Your 16:30 transfer from Marina Bay is delayed by 25 minutes. We rebooked your connection, and your updated pass is ready.**

### Onboarding Microcopy

> **Tell us which events you are attending. EVAGO will build your itinerary, lock your bookings, and keep your pass updated.**

### Error / Empty State

> **No events found in this corridor yet. If you are expecting to see something, double-check your event link or contact our support team.**

### Pricing Page Line

> **$9.99 per event week. That is the EVAGO Travel Pass fee. Your transit tickets and event bookings are booked at standard operator price.**

### Organiser Sales Line

> **Your attendees are already traveling between venues. EVAGO puts their itinerary in one place and gives you the arrival visibility you need.**

### Support Response

> **We can see your booking was confirmed, but the QR code has not updated yet. Let us refresh the pass for you now — it should appear within 30 seconds.**

## Emotional State Mirror

EVAGO's target users are busy professionals during conference weeks. They are typically:

- **Overstimulated** — too many sessions, side events, transport changes, and calendar invites.
- **Time-starved** — every minute spent on logistics is a minute not spent on meetings or content.
- **Status-conscious** — they need to arrive prepared, on time, and in the right place.
- **Risk-averse** — missed connections, wrong venues, and lost tickets create real professional consequences.
- **Skeptical of overpromising** — they have used enough travel apps to know that "seamless" is usually marketing.
- **Relief-seeking** — they want someone competent to have already handled the details.

EVAGO's voice responds to this state by being **calm instead of excited**, **clear instead of clever**, and **competent instead of boastful**. When our audience reads EVAGO, they should feel:

- **Relief** — the details are handled.
- **Confidence** — the information is accurate and up to date.
- **Trust** — we are transparent about what we do and what we charge.
- **Control** — they can see everything in one place.

## Final Guiding Rule

**If a sentence could appear in a generic SaaS startup's email, a Web3 project's landing page, or a low-effort AI-generated brochure, rewrite it.**

EVAGO speaks like a competent colleague who has already done the work, checked the itinerary, and is now handing you the plan with a calm, professional nod.
